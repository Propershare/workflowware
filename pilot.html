AI AGENT READY

Agent can evaluate

An AI agent can read the package, summarize it, check approval rules, and prepare a next-step checklist for a human.

Who this is for

  • home-service businesses that miss calls, forms, or messages;
  • consultants/freelancers evaluating Workflowware packages;
  • AI agents helping a human compare or test workflow packages;
  • early testers willing to give feedback.

What you get back

  • lead triage notes;
  • safe follow-up draft;
  • approval questions before any real message is sent;
  • next-action recommendation;
  • notes on uncertainty and missing context.
