# Origin and Maintenance Note

`workflowware.org` is maintained by BlackLabRats as a public category/spec home for **Workflowware** and **Agentic Workflow Distribution**.

## Maintainer

BlackLabRats

## Foundation

This work is backed by a local Hermes + MAAT Runtime / gitMaat artifact system that packages research, workflow specs, human-facing artifacts, evals, approval rules, and agent handoffs into durable records.

## What we claim

We are defining and practicing the artifact layer for agentic workflow distribution.

## What we do not claim

We do not claim to have invented agents, prompts, skills, automation templates, SaaS, or workflow tools.

## Why publish this

The purpose of `workflowware.org` is to make the category legible and timestamp the public definition:

> Workflowware is software packaged as agent-operable workflows.
