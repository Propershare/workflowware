Revenue-first backlog.

  • 2. Review Response & Reputation Recovery — draft replies; never post without approval.
  • 3. Estimate Follow-Up — revive cold quotes with safe next-touch drafts.
  • 4. Inbox Triage — classify and route messages with escalation rules.
  • 5. Customer Intake Cleanup — normalize messy intake into usable records.
  • 6. Content Repurposing — turn one approved source into channel drafts.
