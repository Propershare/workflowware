# Workflow Spec

## Trigger

A business has a missed lead from a call, web form, text, email, DM, or CRM export.

## Goal

Produce triage notes, a safe follow-up draft, approval questions, and a next-action recommendation.

## Inputs

- business type;
- lead message;
- when received;
- whether anyone replied;
- preferred tone;
- business constraints.

## Finish line

A human can approve, edit, or reject the proposed next action.

## Non-goals

- no automatic message sending;
- no live account access during first test;
- no guarantees of booked jobs or revenue.
