# Approval Rules and Safety Boundaries

## Default autonomy level

Start at **human-approved draft mode**.

The agent may draft, classify, summarize, and recommend. Live sending/booking requires explicit client-approved rules.

## Human approval required for

- first week of all outgoing customer messages;
- pricing or financing questions;
- emergency cases;
- complaints or warranty issues;
- commercial/high-value jobs;
- out-of-area exceptions;
- any uncertain booking action;
- tool-data conflicts.

## Agent may not

- quote firm prices without approved pricing policy;
- diagnose complex issues;
- guarantee arrival times outside calendar rules;
- promise emergency dispatch;
- handle refunds or credits;
- request sensitive payment information;
- use credentials supplied in chat;
- bypass human escalation rules.

## Approved low-risk messages

Example missed-call response:

> Sorry we missed your call — this is [Business]. What service do you need help with, and what ZIP code are you located in?

Example missing-info request:

> Thanks — to route this correctly, can you confirm the property ZIP code and whether this is urgent today?

Example human handoff:

> Thanks, I’m going to have a team member review this and follow up because it may need special handling.

## Emergency escalation

Escalate immediately if message mentions flooding, electrical hazard, gas smell, no AC/heat with vulnerable occupant, active roof leak during storm, fire/smoke, injury, threats, or legal action.

## Audit trail

Every case should record input/source, classification, action taken/drafted, human approval status, escalation reason, final outcome, and notes for eval improvement.
