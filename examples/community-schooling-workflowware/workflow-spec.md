# Workflow Spec — Community Schooling

## Trigger

A community member (or newcomer referred by the community) is stuck: too many links, unclear what MAAT vs Workflowware means, or they need a first finishable job.

## Goal

Produce a short path: orientation + **one** package + schooling step + help ask.

## Inputs

- who they are (operator, student, builder, business owner);
- what they’re trying to finish this week;
- whether they can use sample data only;
- whether they already have a Workflowware ZIP or MAAT sign-in.

## New workflow

1. Orient: MAAT governs; Workflowware installs.  
2. Pick one starter (default: Missed Lead Recovery).  
3. Point to schooling when they want curriculum depth (`#portal`).  
4. Draft help ask for a human (email) — what they tried, what blocked them.  
5. Stop before live credentials or live send.

## Finish line

They know their next click and how to ask for help.

## Non-goals

- selling; paywalls; community “tiers”;
- replacing the member portal;
- shipping Gov Secure Release as the default starter.
