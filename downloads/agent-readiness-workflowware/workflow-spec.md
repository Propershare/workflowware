# Workflow Spec

## Trigger

A business owner realizes AI agents might be giving wrong answers about their business — or giving no answers at all. They want to fix it but do not know where to start.

## Goal

Audit the business's digital presence from an AI agent's perspective. Produce `llms.txt` and `source_of_truth.md`. Deliver a gap report with prioritized fixes.

## Inputs

- Business name
- Business address (physical or service area)
- Business phone number
- Business website URL
- Business category / what they actually do
- Hours of operation
- Social media profiles (optional)
- Review platform links (optional)

## Finish line

The business owner has:

1. An Agent Readiness Score (0-100) with category breakdown
2. `llms.txt` — machine-readable business facts, ready to upload
3. `source_of_truth.md` — verified business information in one document
4. A gap report listing contradictions, missing data, and agent-blind spots
5. A prioritized fix list — what to update first and where

## Non-goals

- No automated posting to business listings
- No SEO audit or keyword research
- No paid advertising recommendations
- No website rebuild or redesign
- No content creation or social media management
- No guarantees of ranking improvement — agents use many signals, and this package only ensures they have correct data
