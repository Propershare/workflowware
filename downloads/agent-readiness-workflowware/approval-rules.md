# Approval Rules

Human approval is required before:

- Publishing or sharing any audit output
- Uploading `llms.txt` to a website
- Sharing `source_of_truth.md` with any third party
- Updating, posting, or modifying any business listing
- Creating accounts on any platform on behalf of the business
- Contacting any platform, directory, or review site
- Recommending paid tools, services, or agencies (only if owner explicitly requests)

The agent may search, compare, flag, and recommend. The human decides what to publish, update, or purchase.

## Why this matters

Agent Readiness is about truth infrastructure. If the agent posts wrong information to fix wrong information, it has made the problem worse. Every output must be reviewed by the business owner before it leaves their hands.
