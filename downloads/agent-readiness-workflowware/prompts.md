# Agent Readiness Prompts

Free. No gate. No funnel. Use these.

---

## Intake Interview

```
I want to make sure AI agents have correct information about my business. Ask me one question at a time to collect: my business name, physical address or service area, phone number, website URL, hours of operation, what my business actually does (category/services), and any social media or review profiles I want checked. Do not move to the next question until I answer the current one. After collecting all information, summarize what you have and ask me to confirm before searching.
```

---

## Multi-Platform Audit

```
Search for [BUSINESS NAME] across Google, Bing, Yelp, Apple Maps, Facebook, and [BUSINESS WEBSITE]. For each platform, capture: the business name as displayed, the address, the phone number, the hours of operation, the business category, and the website link. Compare every field against the verified facts I provided. Flag every contradiction with the platform name and the specific discrepancy. Also flag anything that is missing entirely — no hours listed, no phone number, no website link. Produce a contradiction table and a missing-data table.
```

## Agent Readiness Score

```
Score my business's agent readiness across six categories. For each category, show what you found, what's correct, what's wrong, and the score.

Categories:
- Name consistency (20 points): Is the business name identical across all platforms?
- Address accuracy (20 points): Is the address correct and consistent?
- Phone consistency (15 points): Is the phone number the same everywhere?
- Hours accuracy (15 points): Are hours correct and present on all platforms?
- Website structure (15 points): Does the website have structured data, clear navigation, and correct business info?
- Category/relevance (15 points): Is the business categorized correctly so agents understand what it does?

Total: XX/100. Show the breakdown. Then tell me the top 3 fixes that would raise my score the most.
```

## llms.txt Generator

```
Generate an llms.txt file for [BUSINESS NAME] using the llms.txt standard. Include:

- Business name
- Brief description (1-2 sentences about what the business does)
- Physical address or service area
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Hours of operation
- Business category
- Social media profile URLs (if provided)
- A note that this file was generated by Agent Readiness Workflowware and should be reviewed by a human before publishing

Format it as plain text following the llms.txt specification. The file should be ready to upload to the root of the business website.
```
