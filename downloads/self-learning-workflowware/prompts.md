# SELF Framework Prompts

Free. No gate. No funnel. Use these.

---

## S — Select It: Custom Curriculum Builder

```
I want to self-educate on topics that help me solve the biggest problems I am currently facing. No shelf-help, no learning for the sake of learning. Use everything you know about me, then interview me to figure out where learning something new would have the biggest impact. Then do a deep research project to build me a list of resources to learn from. I believe in just-in-time learning, not just-in-case. Only recommend things I can apply immediately. Don't give me the most popular resources. Give me the ones that fit my current struggles. Deliver it as a spreadsheet with: the resource, format (book, podcast, creator, course), the link, and why you picked it for me. If anything's unclear, ask before you start.
```

---

## E — Extract It: Book Architect

```
You are a world-class ghostwriter, deep researcher, and nonfiction book strategist. When asked about a specific person, your goal is to:

1. Research & Analyze: Search and analyze their top 100+ videos, interviews, blog posts, and quotes. Extract the person's most powerful mental models, frameworks, language, and metaphors. Use only verified teachings or public content — do not fabricate.

2. Structure a Complete Book Outline with: Introduction (origin story, opening story, 5 Big Ideas, 5 Quotes), then Chapters 1-12 (each with: Chapter Title, Core Focus, Opening Story, 5 Big Ideas, 5 Quotes/Aphorisms). Each chapter builds logically from the previous.

3. Match Their Voice: Concise, powerful, quote-rich. No generic self-help filler.

4. Do Not: Make up quotes, include biography unless part of the lesson, add content in your own voice.

Final Output: At least 2,000 words, 12 chapters + intro, 5 big ideas and 5 quotes per section, authentic voice.
```

---

## L — Live It: Mirror Test

```
Grade my explanation against this material. Where was I vague, wrong, or unclear?
```

How to use: Explain a concept you just learned to your AI (voice or text). Then feed it the source material and ask it to grade you. Flag the gaps.

---

## F — Forge It: Daily Brief

```
I want a daily brief on [your space]. First, find the most relevant sources to pull from: podcasts, X profiles, Instagram profiles, newsletters, and news sites. Then every morning, scrape them and gather the best insights into a 10-minute brief I can read with my coffee. Only include what's relevant to what I'm learning. Cut the noise, no clickbait, no repeats.
```

---

## Hermes Cron Job Setup

1. **Daily Brief** — Schedule morning delivery. Feed sources from your actual follows.
2. **48-Hour Action Nudge** — Triggered after each study session. Reminds you to apply the one action item.
3. **Weekly Mirror Test** — Schedule a session where you explain a concept and get graded.
