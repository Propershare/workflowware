Agentic Workflow Distribution

Workflowware™ is software packaged as agent-operable workflows.

Portable workflow packages with instructions, tools, evals, approval rules, memory pointers, and human-facing documentation so agents can install, operate, evaluate, and improve real work.

Prompts are ingredients. Agents are workers. Workflowware is the operating package.

Apps give people tools. Workflowware gives agents and humans a governed job package.

Workflowware should be free to understand and start building. Implementation is scoped by workflow complexity, integrations, approval requirements, risk, and ongoing support needs.

Origin note

Workflowware.org is maintained by BlackLabRats / propershare as a public category/spec home for Workflowware™ and Agentic Workflow Distribution.

Interim trademark position: Until a federal trademark application is funded and filed, use Workflowware™ — not Workflowware®. The goal is to publish a clear usage trail without overclaiming registration.

We do not claim to have invented agents, prompts, skills, automation templates, or SaaS. The claim is narrower: the missing layer is the governed, agent-operable workflow artifact that packages the job itself.
